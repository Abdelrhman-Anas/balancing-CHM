Alchemists' Crucible

What is it?

The Alchemists' Crucible is a chemistry tool that can predict chemical equations with high Accuracy giving you almost the right equation balanced and with its stoichiometry calculator.

How to Use

using this tool is just so simple and stright forward.
1) choose what type of reaction you want
2) put your reactants, two or just one reactant for decomposition
make sure you write the correct Molecular formula with all its numbers and parentheses like this "Fe2(CO3)3"
3) submit your reactants to the tool and enjoy you reaction

Accuracy
[98%]
Scope

Capabilities

Predicts products for the four major reaction types, handles stoichiometry for basic elemental interactions, and provides real-time balanced chemical formulas.

Limitations

Currently limited to inorganic transformations. Complex organometallic pathways and advanced redox balancing beyond basic displacement are not supported in the current forge version.
